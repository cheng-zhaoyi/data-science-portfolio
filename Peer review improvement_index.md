## Projects

### 🌍 Global Land Temperature Analysis (1750–2015)

**Research question:** Has the Earth's land surface warmed over the last two and a half centuries, and has the rate of warming accelerated in recent decades?

Using \~265 years of monthly global land-surface temperature data (Berkeley Earth), I aggregated the record into annual means, computed temperature anomalies against the 1961–1990 baseline, and estimated warming rates by linear regression across different periods.


**What the visualisation shows:** after a long period of relatively small fluctuation, land temperature rises sharply from around the 1980s onward. Besides linear‑regression decadal‑rate results, a quadratic dashed curve captures non‑linear accelerated warming. A red annotation marks the hottest year within the dataset. The fitted linear trend indicates **+0.047 °C per decade** across the full record, accelerating to **+0.27 °C per decade** since 1975 (roughly 4× faster). The 2010s are the warmest decade on record.
